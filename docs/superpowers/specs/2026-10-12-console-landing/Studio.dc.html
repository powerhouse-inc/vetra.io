How a session goes

  1. 01 BriefDescribe the process, paste a spreadsheet, or point at an existing package.
  2. 02 PlanStudio proposes models and operations. Nothing is written until you approve.
  3. 03 BuildModels, reducers, editors and tests, committed step by step.
  4. 04 PreviewUse the app live inside your studio while it is being built.
  5. 05 PublishVersion it to the registry and roll it out to a Cloud environment.
